Rules will let the business set channel policy without a code change: hold a few units back from eMAG, prefer one channel when stock runs low, or stop selling a SKU below a threshold. This version uses one fixed rule, the first order to reach the warehouse wins, so the conflict path stays easy to follow and every exception still reaches a person.

Roles will separate who can watch from who can decide: support staff read orders and the audit log, operators work the review queue, admins change rules. Real sign-in is out of scope for this proof of concept, so everyone who opens the dashboard acts as the operator, and every decision is recorded under that name.
